Place the executable iperf3 binary for ABI armeabi-v7a in this folder.

Required final filename:
iperf3

Example final path:
android/app/src/main/assets/iperf3/armeabi-v7a/iperf3

Do not rename it to iperf3.exe, iperf3.bin, or iperf3.txt.
The file must be a real Android/Linux executable for armeabi-v7a.
